Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Data Controller

Nexum Trade Consulting, managed by Juan Oviedo and Facundo Oviedo. Address: Buenos Aires, Argentina. Contact: info@nexumtc.com.ar.

2. Information Collected and Purpose

Through our contact form and direct communication channels (WhatsApp and email), we may collect information such as: first and last name, email address, telephone number, company name, and commercial industry.

The collection of this data serves the exclusive purpose of:

  • Responding to commercial inquiries and export feasibility evaluation requests.
  • Assessing regulatory, tariff, and sanitary requirements for your products.
  • Drafting tailored consulting proposals for prospective clients.

3. Confidentiality, Security & Storage

Nexum Trade Consulting applies reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Data is securely stored in cloud database infrastructure provided by Supabase Inc., with standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Data is stored on servers of Supabase Inc., which may be located outside the Argentine Republic. By submitting the contact form and accepting this policy, the user consents to such international transfer. We do not sell, rent, or transfer contact details to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Your Rights (Access, Rectification, Deletion)

Under Argentine Law 25,326, data subjects have the right to access, update, rectify, or request the deletion of their personal information at any time.

To exercise these rights, please submit a written request with proof of identity to our official email address: info@nexumtc.com.ar.

5. Regulatory Authority

The Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP), as the supervisory authority of Law No. 25,326, has the power to handle complaints and claims related to non-compliance with data protection regulations in Argentina.
