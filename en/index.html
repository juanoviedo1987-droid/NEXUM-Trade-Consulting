Frequently Asked Questions

How does Nexum's service differ from a traditional customs broker?
A customs broker executes clearance at port. Nexum designs and directs the strategy beforehand and afterward: commercial vetting, export pricing, logistics, and coordination with partner agents (SENASA/FDA) and licensed brokers.
How do we ensure secure foreign currency collection?
We structure transactions backed by international banking instruments (irrevocable Letters of Credit, advance deposits, documentary collections), thoroughly vetting buyer creditworthiness beforehand.
How does the Export Diagnostic work and how is it paid?
We assess technical, tariff, and regulatory feasibility, delivering a Feasibility Report in 1 to 2 weeks. We send the formal proposal within 24 business hours.
Do you also advise on importing machinery or raw materials?
Yes. We advise on strategic imports of capital goods, supplies, and raw materials: supplier vetting, tariff classification, and landed cost simulations; the import diagnostic is custom-quoted according to scope.
