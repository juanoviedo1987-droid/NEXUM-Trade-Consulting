Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before taking your first step into foreign markets.

Does my company need an internal export department to start selling abroad?
No. That is precisely one of the main advantages of partnering with Nexum: we act as your outsourced international trade department. We coordinate market research, compliance, logistics, and cross-border negotiations without burdening your company with specialized payroll overhead.
How does Nexum's service differ from a traditional customs broker?
A customs broker is an indispensable partner focused strictly on formal port clearance. Nexum designs and directs the strategy beforehand and afterward: validating international buyers, structuring competitive export pricing, advisory and coordination with partner agents on sanitary clearances (SENASA/FDA), drafting agreements, and international logistics to maximize operational success and security.
How do we ensure secure foreign currency collection?
We structure payment protocols backed by international banking instruments (irrevocable Letters of Credit, advance deposits, documentary collections) and thoroughly vet the financial standing of the buyer beforehand to mitigate default risks to the minimum.
What are the minimum requirements my product must meet for export evaluation?
Primarily having an active manufacturing registration in your home country and sufficient production capacity to supply additional export volume. In our initial assessment, we verify whether your product requires labeling adjustments, destination sanitary registrations, or quality certifications.
How does the Export Diagnostic work and how is it paid?
The Export Diagnostic is a paid professional service where we evaluate the technical, tariff, and regulatory feasibility of your product, delivering a comprehensive Feasibility Report within an estimated timeframe of 1 to 2 weeks. Upon submitting your inquiry via our form, we analyze your company's profile and send you the diagnostic proposal within 24 business hours. Payment is due prior to starting the evaluation.
Do you also advise on importing machinery or raw materials?
Yes. We provide strategic import consulting for manufacturing and productive SMEs looking to acquire capital goods (machinery and technology) or essential raw materials and inputs. We handle supplier verification at origin, tariff classification, landed cost simulations at destination plant, regulatory compliance, and international logistics coordination to boost your operational competitiveness and profitability. For strategic import projects, the diagnostic is custom-quoted according to the operational scope.
